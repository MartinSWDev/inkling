# Swipe card layout: question up top, hint above the card, picture clear, title below

Rounds 2 and 4 (and the same idea for round 3 pairs where it applies).

1. Question at the top: move the round question ("Would you get stuck into this?" / "Would you want to find out?") out of the card to a paper tag under the top bar, centred, Fraunces ~22px. It stays put for the whole round (it doesn't move with the card). Remove the question tag from the card.
2. Swipe hint above the card: a small row directly above the card: "← Not for me" on the left, "Yes →" on the right (round 4 also "↑ Tell me more" centred, matching the ★ button). Ink text at 70%, 14px, with small arrow icons. Animate it once when the round starts (arrows nudge left/right) and fade it to 40% after the second swipe. The "1 of 5" counter sits in the same row, centred (or under the ↑ hint in round 4).
3. Picture clear: the card art fills the whole card with NO text overlaid (remove the paper label from the image). Keep the drag stamps (Yes / Not for me) — they only appear while dragging.
4. Title below the card: the activity/question title ("Taking apart an old radio", "Why do we dream?") sits directly under the card on its own paper strip the card's width, Fraunces ~20px, and changes with each card (crossfade). The ✕/★/✓ buttons stay in their fixed row below that.
5. Keep the desk top, bed and shelves visible above the stack: if space is tight, reduce the card art height (16:10 → 16:11 is fine) rather than covering the desk. Check at 390×844, 360×780 and the desktop frame; nothing overlaps and the order top→bottom is: top bar, question tag, room (visible), hint row, card, title strip, buttons.
6. Pairs (round 3): question "Which is more you?" also moves to the top tag; pair cards show the picture with no overlaid label and the label + caption below each picture.
