Inkling

Inkling is a free game that helps UK school leavers find the degree subjects that fit them. Play four quick rounds of cards, then see your top 3 subjects, a wildcard, real UK courses and apprenticeships — no sign-up.

Built with Meta models:

  • Muse Spark Open, running on Baseten — wrote and reviewed the subject cards, and writes and plays the live simulated students.
  • Muse Spark (Meta Model API) — writes your personal inkling note, live.
  • Muse Image — drew the room, stickers, card art and your paper pal.
  • SAM 3.1 — cut out and checked every object.
  • Muse Code — the model that built this site.
